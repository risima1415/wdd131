Website Plan

Site Name

Interior Harmony Designs

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase professional interior design services, highlight past projects, display a portfolio, and allow potential clients to get in contact for consultations.

Target Audience

Homeowners, business owners, and property developers who are looking for modern, stylish, and professional interior design services.

Color Schema

Primary Color – Beige (#F5F5DC):
Used for background highlights, section blocks, wireframe boxes, and soft visual areas.

Secondary Color – Brown (#8B4513):
Used for headings, accents, borders, and highlighted text areas.

Typography

Heading Font: Montserrat — clean, modern, and strong for titles.
Body Font: Lato or Open Sans — easy to read and professional-looking.

Site Map

  • Home Page
  • About Page
  • Services Page
  • Projects / Portfolio Page
  • Contact Page
